News river: Valor International, CNBC, MarketWatch and NYT direct every 60 s; Guardian, BBC, MercoPress, Rio Times and Google News through free relays every 10 min. Session status ignores exchange holidays. For monitoring only, not for execution or pricing. Search: type a ticker and press GO.
